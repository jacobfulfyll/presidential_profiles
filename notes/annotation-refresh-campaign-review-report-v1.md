The ledger continues to store separate atomic events. A versioned bundle controls
prompt, context, response schema, and decoding. A versioned campaign locks one
corpus, exact bundle versions, exact persisted subject selections, exact model
receipts, reasoning effort, evaluation rules, and promotion policy, then expands
into independent child runs.

The thin orchestrator delegates:

- assignments and ingestion to the generic terminal workflow;
- sealing, comparison, adjudication, promotion, and materialization to the Phase 1
  ledger;
- bundle/campaign selection and aggregate status to
  `annotation_refresh.py`.

Successful children remain valid when a sibling fails.

## Why constituency is combined but atomic

Constituency and named-adversary analysis use the same paragraph, bounded
same-speech context, group/entity recognition, and stance reasoning. One combined
pass avoids retransmitting those inputs for seven separate label types.

They remain semantically independent. A paragraph may claim a constituency without
an adversary, name an adversary without claiming a constituency, do both, do
neither, or assign one literal group different roles. The raw constituency object
has its own atomic spec and label group, and no decoder reverses
`enemy_naming` to infer it.

## Reversible versus irreversible later actions

Reversible:

- Stage 1 code, registry, documentation, and tests;
- draft plans and unsealed abandoned work;
- derived token estimates and temporary materialization candidates.

Irreversible or append-only:

- model token expenditure;
- sealing a run or campaign;
- publishing an adjudication;
- publishing a promotion.

A promotion can be reversed only by a new transition.
