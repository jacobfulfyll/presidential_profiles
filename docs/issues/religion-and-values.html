In their words

Early

“I reciprocate your kind prayers for the protection and blessing of the common Father and Creator of man, and tender you for yourselves and your religious association, assurances of my high respect and esteem.”

Thomas Jefferson, Response to Danbury Baptist Association, 1802
At its peak

“The men of Normandy had faith that what they were doing was right, faith that they fought for all humanity, faith that a just God would grant them mercy on this beachhead or on the next.”

Ronald Reagan, 40th Anniversary of D-Day, 1984
Most recent

“In America, we celebrate faith, we cherish religion, we lift our voices in prayer, and we raise our sights to the Glory of God.”

Donald Trump, State of the Union Address, 2020
