Broad trend

Each point is the share of eligible paragraphs assigned to this broad CorEx issue in a five-year period. The shaded 95% sampling band resamples whole speeches; a dotted line marks periods too thin to trust.Each dot is one president's own share inside one 5-year period when at least 25 eligible paragraphs support that cell.

Explain this measure

Confidence interval — A range produced by repeatedly resampling whole speeches.

A 7% estimate with a 4–10% interval says repeat samples often land in that range.

  1. Resample speeches
  2. Recalculate the estimate
  3. Keep the middle 95%
Formula and limitations

bootstrap quantiles 2.5% and 97.5% · measure's native unit

It is not a 95% probability that this one fixed interval contains truth.

Explain this measure

Share of paragraphs — The number of labeled paragraphs divided by every paragraph in the selected record.

Twenty labeled paragraphs among 100 total paragraphs is 20%.

  1. Count paragraphs carrying the label
  2. Count every eligible paragraph
  3. Divide the labeled count by the total
Formula and limitations

labeled paragraphs / eligible paragraphs · percent of paragraphs

Paragraphs vary greatly in length, and AI labels can disagree.
