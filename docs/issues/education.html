Share of presidential speech about education. The shaded band is a 95% interval from a bootstrap that resamples whole speeches, so it widens where a period rests on a handful of them; a dotted line marks periods too thin to trust. Each dot is one president's own share inside one 5-year period, for the 25-paragraph-and-up cells where they said enough to measure. These labels come from a deterministic topic model with no AI annotator in the loop, so the band covers sampling error only.

In their words

Early

“Amongst the motives to such an Institution, the assimilation of the principles, opinions and manners of our Country men, but the common education of a portion of our Youth from every quarter, well deserves attention.”

George Washington, Eighth Annual Message to Congress, 1796
At its peak

“We have to ask more in our schools of our students, our teachers, our principals, our parents.”

Bill Clinton, Address Before a Joint Session of Congress, 1993
Most recent

“These teachers - and I've seen them at a lot of schools where they had problems - these teachers love their students.”

Donald Trump, Remarks at the Conservative Political Action Conference, 2018
