Deterministic broad axis

Broad trend

Each point is the share of eligible paragraphs assigned to this broad CorEx issue in a five-year period. The shaded 95% sampling band resamples whole speeches; a dotted line marks periods too thin to trust. A hollow ring marks a point whose interval could not be resolved at all — every speech in that period agreed exactly, on too few speeches for the agreement to mean anything. Its share is plotted; its uncertainty is unknown, which is not the same as small. Each dot is one president's own share inside one 5-year period when at least 25 eligible paragraphs support that cell.

Explain this measure

Confidence interval — A range produced by repeatedly resampling whole speeches.

A 7% estimate with a 4–10% interval says repeat samples often land in that range.

  1. Resample speeches
  2. Recalculate the estimate
  3. Keep the middle 95%
Formula and limitations

bootstrap quantiles 2.5% and 97.5% · measure's native unit

It is not a 95% probability that this one fixed interval contains truth.

Early window · 1790–1830

“But going into a state of agriculture, it may be as advantageous to a society, as it is to an individual, who has more land than he can improve, to sell a part, and lay out the money in stocks and implements of agriculture, for the better improvement of the residue.”

Thomas Jefferson, Address to Brother Handsome Lake, 1802 /the-presidency/presidential-speeches/november-3-1802-address-brother-handsome-lake · paragraph 5

“The departure of the Farm Board from its original purpose by making loans to farmers' cooperatives to preserve prices from panic served an emergency, but such an action in normal times is absolutely destructive of the farmers' own interest.”

Explain this measure

Share of paragraphs — The number of labeled paragraphs divided by every paragraph in the selected record.

Twenty labeled paragraphs among 100 total paragraphs is 20%.

  1. Count paragraphs carrying the label
  2. Count every eligible paragraph
  3. Divide the labeled count by the total
Formula and limitations

labeled paragraphs / eligible paragraphs · percent of paragraphs

Paragraphs vary greatly in length, and AI labels can disagree.
