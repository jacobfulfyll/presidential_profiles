30-second verdict

Useful evidence—with explicit boundaries

This audit asks whether the site’s conclusions survive different instruments and whether every denominator is visible. It does not claim that agreement between two related language models is human-validated accuracy.

8,570 paired paragraph judgments make broad reproducibility checks possible.
κ 0.49 zero-sum judgments are materially model-sensitive despite 94.4% raw agreement.
32,531 speaker-audited paragraphs are eligible for actual-speaker analysis; other pages may use explicitly different populations.

What changed in this audit: version 2 repairs the missing-row calculation, restricts entity comparisons to genuinely paired paragraphs, normalizes and deduplicates topics before counting, expresses interval widths in percentage points, and orders every era chronologically.

Technical evidence · 1

94.4% agreement can still hide unstable positives

Zero-sum framing is rare. Both models usually say “no,” so raw agreement is dominated by easy shared negatives. Cohen’s κ adjusts for chance agreement under the observed label prevalence and falls to 0.49.

Technical evidence · 2

Finding the same name is harder than judging its stance

Entity evaluation has two stages. First ask whether both models extracted the same normalized name; only then ask whether their stance judgments agree.

Case and whitespace normalization is deliberately conservative; it does not silently collapse aliases such as a title and a full name.

Technical evidence · 3

Two surfaces, two uncertainty budgets

These widths are displayed in percentage points. CorEx uses five-year periods; LLM topics use nine broad Story eras. Their averages are shown separately because different temporal grains and base rates make a cross-surface bar comparison misleading.

The disagreement component does not capture shared model bias, taxonomy error, or within-speech correlation in the half-gap. It assumes disagreement in the sampled documents transfers to full-corpus era estimates.

Technical evidence · 4

Coverage and label behavior constrain every finding

After canonical case normalization and within-paragraph topic deduplication, the primary pass contains 52,133 assignments and 14,507 multi-label paragraphs. 404 paragraphs carry no topic and remain in denominators. The project also marks 3 presidents with fewer than five source documents rather than ranking them as precise outliers.

Derived locally

Build contract

data-quality-v2 performs no model calls. It validates semantic key sets, source hashes, populations, units, and canonical agreement values before publication.

Frozen inputs

Model evidence

Each annotation row retains its run ID. The public manifest records model, prompt version, prompt hash, source hash, and the deterministic bootstrap recipe.

Not yet measured

Human validity

Cross-model reproducibility can reveal sensitivity, not truth. A blinded, adjudicated human study is still required for model-to-human validity estimates. The execution-blocked protocol receipt publishes its design and gates without exposing coder assignments.

Processing and interpretation checklist
  1. Join paragraphs only on (doc_name, para_idx), with uniqueness and key-set validation.
  2. Normalize taxonomy labels against the frozen taxonomy and reject unknown values.
  3. Keep missing, empty, excluded, and thin records visible.
  4. Bootstrap whole speeches rather than treating paragraphs as independent.
  5. Use the metric appropriate to each output structure and show its denominator.
  6. Do not translate cross-model disagreement into accuracy without human labels.
