How we chose the eras

The optimizer proposes persistent rhetorical regimes. Sensitivity tests show what survives alternate reasonable choices. Historical evidence resolves exact dates without pretending that an algorithm discovered the only possible history.

The 30-second verdict

Under the declared 9-era, 3-cycle-unit-minimum specification, the primary mathematical starts are 1789, 1805, 1849, 1869, 1913, 1937, 1953, 1981, 2017. The Story makes 2 modeled-start adjustments—1805→1809, 1937→1933—and represents the grid-labeled 1849 unit with the editorial start 1850. Together, those choices add only 0.512% to within-era variation.

Conditional, not inevitable

9 eras is an editorial product constraint. The audit tests 2–12 segments, duration, grid, weighting, endpoint, and axis omissions.

Stable center, sensitive edges

24 conditions are estimable. Their adjusted-Rand scores and exact starts are published—not compressed into a single confidence claim.

The present is provisional

The final 2025–2026 cycle is incomplete. Excluding it moves the last modeled start, so the 2017–2026 Story era is explicitly right-censored.

How the close calls were resolved

1809 · Madison takes office

The primary optimum is 1805. Moving the cut to the reviewed start costs 0.058% of the optimum—an explicit historical tie-break inside the measured sensitivity range.

1933 · FDR takes office

The primary 9-era optimum is 1937, while the best two-segment split begins at 1933. The reviewed start costs 0.454% of the optimum.

2017 · provisional endpoint

The full primary run starts at 2017; excluding the incomplete terminal unit starts the last modeled segment at 2013. This is endpoint sensitivity, not proof against genre mix.

Why did 1872 appear before? The retained bin-grain cluster audit places its closest boundary to the Gilded Age at 1872; the four-year primary partition starts at 1869, and the governed Story era starts at 1869. The grains answer different questions, so the legacy result remains downloadable rather than silently rewritten.

Accession-year ownership: a speech keeps its real date, but an outgoing president's speech belongs to the Story regime that presidency closes. This rule changes Story organization only; it never alters the measurements or boundary analysis.

What the model does—and does not do

  1. Fingerprint: 63 topic, combat, register, dictionary-marker, readability/pronoun/modal, and assigned speech-type axes.
  2. Primary specification: z-score each axis across four-year cycles, request exactly 9 contiguous segments, and require at least 3 cycles per segment.
  3. Weighting: the primary gives every nonconstant axis equal weight. A sensitivity run gives every axis family equal aggregate weight.
  4. Support: each segment carries its speech and paragraph counts; the final incomplete cycle carries a right-censor flag.
  5. Statistical status: descriptive periodization of this curated formal-speech corpus. These are neither confidence intervals nor causal estimates.
