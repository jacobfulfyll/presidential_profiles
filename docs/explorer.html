Explain this measure

Rate per 10,000 words — A count divided by all words and scaled to a same-sized 10,000-word container.

Five mentions in 10,000 words equals 5 per 10,000.

  1. Count included words
  2. Count all words
  3. Divide and multiply by 10,000
Formula and limitations

count / words × 10,000 · mentions per 10,000 words

Words can carry different meanings in context.
