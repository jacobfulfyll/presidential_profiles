Methods appendix · model comparison

30-second answer

These are different instruments

CorEx follows inspectable word patterns. The LLM applies a frozen semantic rubric. Agreement supports robustness; mismatch identifies sensitivity.

Shared population

35,825 topic-bearing paragraphs

The preregistered drop_unlabeled=True estimand excludes 404 primary-LLM no-topic paragraphs from 36,229 source paragraphs. Neither model is treated as a gold label.

Scientific result

A prediction failed

Anchor-word coherence did not predict cross-method Jaccard. The failed preregistered expectation remains prominent.

Preregistered result · failed prediction

Cleaner anchor co-occurrence did not imply stronger cross-method agreement

Spearman ρ = -0.054 · two-sided p = 0.850

Across 15 anchored issues, the relationship is essentially absent. Immigration supplies the sharpest counterexample: it has the lowest anchor coherence yet a Jaccard overlap of 0.374. Publishing this failure is evidence of a testable workflow—not evidence that either labeling system is true.

The two-minute visual explanation

CorEx · deterministic local model

15 anchored issue factors

  • Binary word-presence features from the complete paragraph corpus.
  • Declared era-spanning anchors guide correlated-word factors.
  • Strength: repeatable vocabulary evidence.
  • Blind spot: semantic continuity when historical wording changes.

LLM · frozen paid judgments

50 topics in 17 domains

  • Corpus-derived taxonomy frozen before the complete paragraph pass.
  • Text and decade visible; identity and title masked for paragraph judgments.
  • Strength: meaning beyond exact anchor words.
  • Blind spot: rubric interpretation and shared model-family bias.

Named entities are not a CorEx output. Entity detection and stance come from a separate LLM pass and are evaluated as model-to-model reproducibility on Data Quality.

How often each instrument activates an issue

Paired bars share one percentage scale and the same denominator: the 35,825 primary-LLM topic-bearing paragraphs retained by the preregistered comparison estimand. The 404 no-topic paragraphs are excluded. Patterns distinguish the instruments; they do not decide which instrument is correct.

Overlap and chance-adjusted agreement answer different questions

Jaccard asks how much of the positive union overlaps. κ uses the complete yes/no table and corrects for expected agreement from each instrument’s prevalence.
