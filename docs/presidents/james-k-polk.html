Explain this measure

Share of paragraphs — The number of labeled paragraphs divided by every paragraph in the selected record.

Twenty labeled paragraphs among 100 total paragraphs is 20%.

  1. Count paragraphs carrying the label
  2. Count every eligible paragraph
  3. Divide the labeled count by the total

labeled paragraphs / eligible paragraphs · percent of paragraphs

Paragraphs vary greatly in length, and AI labels can disagree.
